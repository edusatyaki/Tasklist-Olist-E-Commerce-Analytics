Section 01
Problem Statement
Core Problem
Olist is Brazil's largest online marketplace connecting thousands of sellers to millions of customers. Between Sep 2016 and Oct 2018, the platform processed 99,441 orders generating R$16 million. Despite strong growth, the business faces challenges: a 3% repeat purchase rate, 6.77% late delivery rate strongly correlated with 1-star reviews, and a Pareto seller problem where 10% of sellers drive 67.5% of revenue. This project builds a full analytical stack to diagnose these issues.
Stakeholders
Primary: Olist Head of Growth, Head of Operations, Customer Success

Secondary: Seller Relations team, Logistics partners, Category managers, BI team
Dataset Context
Source: 9 relational CSV files with shared keys
Metrics: 99,441 orders · 93,357 customers · 3,095 sellers · 32,951 products
